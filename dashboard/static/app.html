Detection thresholds

When ML detections trigger a decoy + redirect: per attack class, at least N flows from one source, each ≥ min confidence, within T seconds, with mean confidence ≥ X. A single flow never triggers. Control-plane traffic (API server, kubelet, kube-system) is never acted on.

With medium or high, every flow at or above that confidence counts and the mean must reach it; each class still needs its N flows in T seconds (a single flow never triggers).
